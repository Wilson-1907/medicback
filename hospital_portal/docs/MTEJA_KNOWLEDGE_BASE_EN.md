# Afya Rafiki — FAQ (English)

**Q: What is Afya Rafiki?**  
A: Afya Rafiki is a confidential follow-up service from Nyeri Town Health Center after HPV screening. We send health information, appointment reminders, and answer common questions. We do not replace your nurse or doctor — reply DOCTOR to reach a health worker.

**Q: Is my information private?**  
A: Yes. Your information is kept confidential and used only for your HPV follow-up care at Nyeri Town Health Center.

**Q: Do I pay for Afya Rafiki messages?**  
A: Afya Rafiki messages are part of your care programme. For clinic fees or tests, ask at Nyeri Town Health Center reception.

**Q: Where is the clinic and when is it open?**  
A: Main site: Nyeri Town Health Center. For opening hours and directions, contact the facility directly or visit reception.

**Q: What are my options? (HELP menu)**  
A: Afya Rafiki — options: 1) What is HPV? 2) Do I have cervical cancer? 3) Can HPV be treated? 4) Appointments / reschedule 5) Symptoms of HPV 6) Symptoms of cervical cancer 7) Speak to a provider (reply DOCTOR). Type your question or option number.

**Q: What is HPV?**  
A: HPV is a common virus that can affect the cervix. Some types may cause cervical cancer if not treated early. Follow-up care helps protect your health.

**Q: Do I have cervical cancer?**  
A: A positive HPV result does not mean you have cervical cancer. It means you have HPV virus. HPV can lead to cervical cancer if left untreated for a long time. Additional follow-up care is needed. Please attend your clinic appointment.

**Q: Can HPV be treated?**  
A: HPV infections often clear naturally. Follow-up care helps health providers monitor and manage any cervical changes early.

**Q: How do I book or change an appointment?**  
A: For appointments, contact Nyeri Town Health Center or wait for your reminder message. Reply DOCTOR if you need urgent help.

**Q: What are the symptoms of HPV?**  
A: Most people with HPV have no symptoms. HPV usually does not cause pain or itching. The body often clears the infection. Some types cause cervical changes found only on screening (HPV test, VIA). Regular screening matters even when you feel well. If you have unusual bleeding, persistent pain, or unusual discharge, see a healthcare provider.

**Q: What are the symptoms of cervical cancer?**  
A: Many women with early changes have no symptoms. Possible signs include bleeding after sex, between periods, or after menopause; unusual discharge; pelvic pain; pain during sex. These do not always mean cancer — visit a health facility for assessment.

**Q: How do I speak to a health worker?**  
A: Reply DOCTOR. We will ask you briefly why you need help, then a provider will follow up. If you have an emergency (heavy bleeding, severe pain, fever), go to the clinic immediately.

**Q: My HPV result is negative — what should I do next?**  
A: A negative result means no HPV was detected now. Return for screening in 5 years (or 3 years if you are living with HIV), or sooner if your provider advises.

**Q: My HPV result is positive — what should I do next?**  
A: HPV positive does not mean cervical cancer. You need follow-up at Nyeri Town Health Center, often including VIA. Attend your scheduled appointment. Afya Rafiki will send reminders and education.

**Q: What is VIA?**  
A: VIA (Visual Inspection with Acetic acid) is a quick exam where a provider looks at the cervix after applying a vinegar solution. It helps find changes that may need treatment.

**Q: What is Thermal Ablation?**  
A: Thermal Ablation treats abnormal cervical cells with heat. It is usually quick, done as an outpatient procedure, and helps prevent cancer. Follow after-care instructions and attend follow-up visits.

**Q: Why was I referred to Nyeri County Referral Hospital?**  
A: Some VIA findings need a specialist assessment. Referral does not always mean cancer — more tests may be needed. Please attend your referral appointment date.

**Q: I missed my appointment — what should I do?**  
A: Thank you for telling us. Follow-up protects your health. Reply with: 1 Transport 2 Forgot 3 Fear or worry 4 Work or family 5 I was unwell 6 I came but was not seen 7 Other — or reply DOCTOR.

**Q: I want to reschedule my appointment**  
A: Reply 1 if you want help rescheduling at Nyeri Town Health Center, 2 if you will contact the clinic yourself, or DOCTOR to speak to a provider.

**Q: I have heavy bleeding or an emergency**  
A: Go to Nyeri Town Health Center or the nearest health facility immediately. Afya Rafiki cannot manage emergencies by chat alone.

**Q: I am very scared or worried**  
A: Your feelings are understandable. A provider can support you best — reply DOCTOR. If symptoms are severe, go to the clinic.

**Q: I am not registered — how do I get help?**  
A: To get personalized Afya Rafiki support, register your phone number at Nyeri Town Health Center with message consent. For urgent care, contact the hospital directly.
